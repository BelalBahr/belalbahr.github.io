About
Engineering-backed PM
building products that scale.

I'm a Senior Product Manager with an M.Sc. in Computer Engineering (AI focus) and a B.Eng. in Mechatronics from Cairo University. I've spent 4+ years at the intersection of product, engineering, and go-to-market — leading 0-to-1 builds in deep-tech environments where the problem space isn't always obvious yet.

My work covers the full PM spectrum: launching B2B SaaS products into new markets, building AI-native enterprise tools, owning end-to-end ERP platforms in manufacturing, and rescuing products with broken unit economics. The approach across all of it is the same: work from first principles, write decisions precisely, and own the trade-offs.

Before product, I led a 23-engineer autonomous driving program, finishing 2nd nationally and top-5 globally. That experience built something most PM roles don't: a real understanding of what cross-functional execution looks like under hard constraints.
